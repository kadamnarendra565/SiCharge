📊 Experiment 04: Financial Plan & Break-Even Analysis

Cost Component Category Amount (INR)

TOTAL VARIABLE COST PER UNIT ₹10,000
📐 Break-Even Calculation Equation:
BEP (Units) = Fixed Costs ÷ (Selling Price − Variable Cost)
BEP = ₹4,50,000 ÷ (₹15,000 − ₹10,000) = ₹4,50,000 ÷ ₹5,000 = 90 Units
• Monthly profit at 150 units = (₹15,000 − ₹10,000) × 150 = ₹7,50,000 / month
• Payback period = ~0.6 months (~18 days) after achieving 150 units run-rate.
