# 🎓 100% Free WordPress Submission Guide (3-Minute Setup)

Here is the exact step-by-step method to get your **SiCharge** project live on a real, free WordPress site with zero cost and zero setup hassle.

---

## ⚡ Method 1: Instant 1-Click WordPress with TasteWP (Recommended — Easiest & 100% Free)

*TasteWP gives you a real, full WordPress website instantly with NO registration, NO email, and NO credit card.*

### Step 1: Create your free WordPress site
1. Open your browser and go to: **[https://tastewp.com](https://tastewp.com)**
2. Check the box *"I agree with Terms"* and click the big button **"Create a site"**.
3. In 5 seconds, it will create a live WordPress site and automatically open the **WordPress Admin Dashboard**.

### Step 2: Add your SiCharge project page
1. In the left menu of WordPress Admin, click **Pages** → **Add New Page**.
2. Set the Page Title: `SiCharge — Silicon Carbide Power Modules`
3. Click the black **`+` (Add block)** icon in the editor.
4. Type **Custom HTML** and click the **Custom HTML** block.
5. Open the file [`wordpress/wordpress-full-page.html`](./wordpress-full-page.html) in your project folder, copy all the code, and paste it directly into the block.
6. Click **Preview** to see your colorful cards, tables, and team details.
7. Click the blue **Publish** button in the top right.

### Step 3: Copy your live link for submission
- Click **"View Page"** or copy the page URL from your browser.
- It will look like: `https://xxxxxx.tastewp.com/sicharge/`
- Submit this link to your professor!

## 🌐 Method 2: Free Site on WordPress.com

1. Go to: **[https://wordpress.com/start](https://wordpress.com/start)**
2. Sign up with Google/Email and choose a free domain (e.g. `sicharge-tsec.wordpress.com`).
3. When prompted for plans, click **"Start with a free site"**.
4. Go to **Pages** → **Add New Page**.
5. Add a **Custom HTML** block.
6. Copy and paste the contents of [`wordpress/wordpress-full-page.html`](./wordpress-full-page.html).
7. Click **Publish**.
